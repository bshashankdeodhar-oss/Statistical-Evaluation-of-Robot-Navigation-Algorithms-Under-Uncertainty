5. Limitations

  • All results are simulation-based and do not imply real-world robot performance.
  • The stochastic model uses simplified Gamma-distributed cost noise and Bernoulli disruptions.
  • Trials on the same map are not fully independent; map-level effects may inflate Type I error.
  • The risk-aware planner uses a heuristic risk function; other formulations would produce different results.
  • Path cost comparisons involving failures use an imputation convention; results are sensitive to this choice.
